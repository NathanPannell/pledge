What this draft shows

Every field the Canada Revenue Agency requires on an official cash donation receipt, laid out for a gift made through Pledge:

  • Statement that it is an official receipt for income tax purposes
  • Charity name and address as on file with the CRA, and its registration number
  • Unique serial number, location issued, date received and date issued
  • Donor name and address
  • Amount of the gift, any advantage, and the eligible amount
  • Signature of an authorized individual, and the CRA’s name and website

The charity of the month issues the real receipt. Amounts shown are in the currency the gift was paid in.
