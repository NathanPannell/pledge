Why now

The money hasn’t disappeared. It moved.

Official development assistance fell in 2024, and several of the largest donors announced deeper cuts for 2025. In the same years, companies poured record sums into AI. A rounding error on an AI budget is a full year of funding for a small development program.

How it works

From a model call to a county statistics office

  1. 1

    Route or pledge

    Point your AI client at the Tributary proxy with one base-URL change, or pledge a fixed share of your AI budget.

  2. 2

    We meter usage

    The proxy reads token counts from each response, prices the call, and records the pledge it owes. Prompts and outputs are never stored.

  3. 3

    Monthly grant

    At month end, pledges are pooled and sent to the project of the month and the standing funds you chose.

  4. 4

    Public ledger

    Every contributor and every transfer is listed with a reference number, so anyone can check where the money went.
