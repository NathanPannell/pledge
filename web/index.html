Questions

Before you start

Do you need access to our accounts?

No. Drop in a card statement and it’s read on your device; only AI charges are kept. If you’d rather not upload each month, connect your card read-only through Plaid.

Is this a fee on our AI spend?

No. It’s a gift you choose and can change or pause any month. Your AI providers bill you exactly as before.

Who gets the money and sends the receipt?

Each gift is paid straight to that month’s registered Canadian charity, which issues your official donation receipt. Pledge never holds the money.

Why one gift a month?

Card fees take a fixed cut of every charge. One monthly invoice, paid by bank transfer, means almost all of your gift reaches the charity.
