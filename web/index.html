How a round-up works

Your AI bill already ends in odd cents.

Discounts and sales tax leave odd cents on every top-up. A US$100 credit purchase comes to US$100.80 at checkout. One checkbox rounds it to US$101.00.

Same charge, same card

The round-up is part of your normal checkout. There’s no second payment and no extra card fee.

Pooled monthly

Round-ups are pooled and sent in one transfer, so almost nothing is lost to fees along the way.

Published in full

Every month’s project, amount and transfer reference is listed below.
