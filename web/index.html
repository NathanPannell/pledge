How it works

Four steps, then it runs itself

Set it up once with your finance team. After that it’s one invoice a month.

  1. 1

    Connect your card

    Link your business card through Plaid. We read transactions and pick out AI charges from 16 vendors, from model APIs to coding tools.

  2. 2

    Add provider keys

    Optionally add Anthropic and OpenAI admin keys to see usage-level cost reports. Keys are encrypted and only used to read costs.

  3. 3

    Set the dial

    Choose a pledge from 0.25% to 5%, on card charges or on usage. We use one or the other, never both, so nothing is counted twice.

  4. 4

    One invoice a month

    Pay it like any vendor invoice. The money goes to our charity partner and every invoice appears on the public ledger.

Questions

What finance teams ask us

Is this a fee on our AI spend?

No. It’s a pledge you set and can change or pause any month. Your AI providers bill you exactly as before. Tributary sends a separate invoice for the share you chose.

What can Tributary see?

Card transactions, read-only, through Plaid, and daily cost totals from the provider reports. We store amounts, dates and descriptions. We never see card numbers, prompts or model outputs.

Why do provider keys need admin access?

Anthropic and OpenAI only publish cost reports to admin keys. We encrypt the key and call nothing but the cost endpoint. Create a dedicated key for Tributary and revoke it any time.

Won’t card and usage spend overlap?

Yes, when your card pays the provider. That’s why the pledge uses card charges, usage, or the larger of the two, never the sum.

Why one monthly invoice?

Card fees take a fixed cut of every charge. One invoice a month, paid by bank transfer, means almost all of the pledge reaches the charity.

Which goal does this serve?

UN Sustainable Development Goal 17, Partnerships for the Goals: mobilizing additional resources and building partnerships between companies and civil society.
