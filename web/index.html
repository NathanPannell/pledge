Where your gift goes

Care, research and recovery across Vancouver

The VGH & UBC Hospital Foundation is Vancouver Coastal Health’s main philanthropic partner. Gifts help fund equipment, research and programs at these places.

Vancouver General Hospital · UBC Hospital

Patient care

Equipment and programs for patients and the teams who look after them, at two of BC’s busiest hospitals.

Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute

Research

Studies that turn into new treatments, from the lab to the bedside, for patients across the province.

G.F. Strong Rehabilitation Centre

Recovery

Rehabilitation for people relearning to walk, talk and live independently after injury or illness.

How a gift is made

From your AI bill to the hospital, once a month

  1. Your team uses AI

    Model APIs, coding assistants and team plans, paid on the company card.

  2. We measure it

    A read-only card connection finds AI charges. Provider cost reports can add usage.

  3. You choose a share

    Most companies give 1%. Change it any month, or pause.

  4. One gift a month

    A single invoice, paid like any vendor. The gift goes to the foundation and is listed below.

Questions

Before you start

Is this a fee on our AI spend?

No. It’s a gift you choose and can change or pause any month. Your AI providers bill you exactly as before.

What can Tributary see?

Card transactions, read-only, and daily cost totals from provider reports. Never card numbers, prompts or model outputs.

Why one gift a month?

Card fees take a fixed cut of every charge. One monthly invoice, paid by bank transfer, means almost all of your gift reaches the foundation.

Who receives the money?

The VGH & UBC Hospital Foundation, a registered Canadian charity (132173063RR0001).
